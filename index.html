Welcome to Karaz

An evening, slowly savored.

Karaz means cherry — a small, sweet thing worth lingering over. That's the whole idea here. We pour care into every bowl of shisha, every glass, and every corner of the room so your night unfolds at its own pace.

From the first cloud of smoke to the last sip, Karaz is a place to gather, unwind, and let the hours stretch.
