Welcome to Karaz

A hookah lounge in Falls Church, VA.

Karaz is Arabic for cherry. We're a hookah lounge and cafe in Falls Church serving shisha from Adalya, Starbuzz, and Al Fakher, alongside coffee, matcha, and desserts. Open Sun–Thu 12 PM–2 AM, Fri & Sat 12 PM–3 AM.

Walk in any day, no reservation needed. Inside: velvet booths, gold mashrabiya walls, and low neon. Out back, a patio with a stone fireplace and covered pergola. 21+, valid ID required.
