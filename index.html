Welcome to Karaz

A hookah lounge in Falls Church, VA.

Karaz is Arabic for cherry. We're a hookah lounge and cafe in Falls Church serving shisha from Adalya, Starbuzz, and Al Fakher, alongside coffee, matcha, and desserts. Open every day, noon to midnight.

Walk in any day, no reservation needed. Inside: velvet booths, gold mashrabiya walls, and low neon. Out back, a patio with a stone fireplace and covered pergola. 21+, valid ID required.
