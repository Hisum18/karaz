Small word, big nights

Karaz is Arabic for cherry — a small, sweet thing worth savoring. That's the whole idea behind the room we built.

The Idea

A hookah lounge and a cafe.

Karaz is as much a cafe as it is a hookah lounge. Alongside premium shisha from Adalya, Starbuzz, and Al Fakher, there's a full menu — espresso and specialty lattes, matcha, ice-cold refreshers, and house-made desserts like the loaded Waffle Stix. Come for a whole evening of hookah, or just a coffee and something sweet.

The Space

A lounge, a game room, and a patio.

Settle into the main lounge — plush navy velvet booths, gold mashrabiya walls, and hanging egg chairs under the neon murals. Shoot a round in the game room, or head out back to the covered patio, warm by the stone fireplace. Three distinct spaces, whichever suits the night.
