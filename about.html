The Idea

Karaz means cherry in Arabic. A lounge in Falls Church.

Plush navy velvet booths, gold mashrabiya walls, low neon lighting, and hanging egg chairs. Out on the patio there's a stone fireplace under a covered pergola. We're open noon to midnight, every day.
